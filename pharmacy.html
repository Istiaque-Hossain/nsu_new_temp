ABOUT ICPS 2026

North South University (NSU), Dhaka, Bangladesh, is pleased to announce the 1st International Conference on Pharmaceutical Sciences (ICPS-2026), 13–15 October 2026. This landmark international event serves as a global platform for academic researchers, industry professionals, clinicians, policymakers, and postgraduate scholars to exchange knowledge and explore emerging trends in pharmaceutical sciences.

ICPS-2026 fosters interdisciplinary dialogue across drug discovery, pharmaceutical technology, clinical pharmacy, regulatory sciences, pharmacology, pharmacognosy, biotechnology, and translational research. Featuring keynote lectures, oral/poster sessions, panel discussions, and industry–academia meetings.
