A.K.M. Atiqur Rahman is a Professor at the Department of Economics and Director of the Economic Research Platform (ERP) at NSU. He holds a PhD in Economics from the University of Houston and has extensive experience in academic leadership, research and policy advisory roles including positions with the World Bank and national ministries. Professor Rahman has been instrumental in shaping economic policy discussions and driving research initiatives at the institutional and national levels.
