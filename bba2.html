A Warm Welcome from the Director

Dr Muhammad Faisol Chowdhury
Associate Professor & Director, BBA Programme

Dear Prospective Students,
You are about to make an exciting and important decision about what and where to study, and we are here to support you in making an informed and confident choice.

I am delighted to introduce the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Programme at North South University (NSU)—an exceptional platform for enhancing your knowledge and sharpening the skills required to thrive in today’s global business landscape. The BBA is one of the most versatile undergraduate degrees, broadening your horizons and unlocking international opportunities.

Our internationally recognised BBA programme is not only the top-ranked but also the largest in the country, accredited by leading industry bodies in recognition of our excellence in business education. We offer a modern, industry-focused Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum within an inclusive and supportive environment, where students are encouraged to question, exchange ideas, express opinions, think critically, and embrace diverse perspectives. Our distinguished faculty members, with global academic and professional experience, instil ethical values and prepare students for the realities of life and career.

We are committed to helping our students maximise their career potential. In addition to interactive classroom learning, we offer a wide range of enriching experiences beyond the classroom, including student exchange programmes abroad, collaborative research, industry visits, training sessions, workshops, business competitions, networking and career events, club and volunteering engagements, and many skill development programmes. These extensive co-curricular and extra-curricular opportunities help our students build essential skills that distinguish them in the job market. We are proud of our strong alumni network, with our graduates excelling across public and private sector organisations worldwide. Our graduates leave with more than a degree—they gain the credentials and confidence for lifetime employability.

I warmly invite you to embark on a transformative and globally attuned learning journey through our BBA, fostering both personal and professional growth. We encourage you to visit our vibrant campus and explore our website to learn more.
Wishing you every success in your journey ahead.

BBA Credit Requirements

You are required to complete 130 credits to earn your BBA degree. The programme follows an open credit system, allowing students to enrol in a minimum of two courses (6 credits) and a maximum of five courses (15 credits) per academic term. You may complete your degree with 127 or 124 credits, depending on whether ENG102 and/or BUS112 were waived at the time of your admission. If neither of these courses was waived, the graduation requirement remains 130 credits.

Dual Concentration/Major

You may complete your BBA with double concentrations/majors by taking an additional three courses / 9 extra credits.

Concentration/Major Areas

Students may choose one or two concentration/major areas from the following nine options:

  • Accounting (ACT)
  • Economics (ECO)
  • Finance (FIN)
  • Human Resource Management (HRM)
  • International Business (INB)
  • Management (MGT)
  • Management Information Systems (MIS)
  • Marketing (MKT)
  • Supply Chain Management (SCM)

BBA Graduation CGPA Requirements

  • A minimum CGPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale
  • A minimum GPA of 2.0 in both the School Core and BBA Core areas
  • A minimum GPA of 2.5 in the concentration/major area

If your CGPA falls below 2.0 in any term, you will be placed on academic probation. Students who remain on probation for three consecutive terms will be subjected to academic dismissal.

Entry Requirements

Bangladeshi Applicants
  • SSC and HSC: A combined GPA of 8.0 (on a 5.0 scale) with a minimum GPA of 3.5 in each examination.
  • GCE O-Levels and A-Levels: A minimum of five O-Level subjects with an average grade point of 2.5, and two A-Level subjects with an average grade point of 2.0. Only one grade of ‘E’ is acceptable across the combined O- and A-Level results. (Grading scale: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1)
  • International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IB-DP): Completion of six subjects with a minimum average grade point of 4.0 out of 7.0, equivalent to a total of 24 points.
  • US High School Diploma: A minimum score of 60%.

Applicants whose academic credentials differ from the categories listed above must obtain an equivalence certificate from the appropriate Education Board. Foundation and/or GED programmes are not recognised for BBA admission at NSU.

Prospective students with alternative academic qualifications are advised to contact the Admissions Office to determine their eligibility.

International Applicants
  • Must have completed 12 years of schooling with a minimum average mark of 60%.
  • Must meet one of the following English language proficiency criteria:
    • The medium of instruction in previous studies was English.
    • Or one of the following minimum scores in recognised tests:
      • SAT: 1100 (on a 1600 scale)
      • IELTS: Overall band score of 6.0
      • TOEFL: PBT 514 / CBT 180 / iBT 72

All academic documents of international applicants must be verified by the Ministry of Education or the relevant Embassy.
