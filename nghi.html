About NGHI

NSU Global Health Institute (NGHI) is a premier center dedicated to interdisciplinary research, training, and policy engagement in global health.

Vision

To achieve equitable health outcomes through innovative research and cross-sectoral collaborations across low- and middle-income countries.

Mission

Foster cutting-edge research, provide high-impact training, and advocate for evidence-based policies to address local and global health challenges.

Voluntary Works

Community Health Camps

Free checkups, screenings, and referrals in underserved communities.

Health Awareness Drives

Behavior change campaigns on nutrition, hygiene, and NCD prevention.

Emergency Response Support

Rapid mobilization of volunteers during floods, heatwaves, and outbreaks.

Youth Volunteer Programs

Student-led outreach and peer education initiatives across local communities.
