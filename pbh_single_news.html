Department Hosts Global Health Symposium

Faculty and students shared research on health systems and policy innovation through collaborative sessions with academics, policymakers, and development partners.

The Department of Public Health at North South University opened its 2026 academic calendar with a high-impact Global Health Symposium focused on practical solutions for resilient health systems in Bangladesh.

The event brought together faculty, students, public health experts, and institutional stakeholders. Sessions addressed disease surveillance, health communication, prevention frameworks, and policy translation for local implementation.

Highlights From the Symposium

  • Interactive plenary sessions on health financing, equity, and community-based interventions.
  • Student poster presentations showcasing applied research on local health priorities.
  • Panel discussions connecting academic training with real-world public health practice.

"This symposium created a direct bridge between evidence, policy, and student learning, which is exactly what modern public health education requires."

Department of Public Health, NSU

Long Description

Through this event, the department reaffirmed its commitment to preparing future public health leaders who can work across research, policy, and field implementation. The discussions and outcomes from the symposium will inform upcoming workshops, collaborative projects, and student engagement activities throughout the year.
