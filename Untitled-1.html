About Our Department

Introduction

The Department of Public Health at NSU is dedicated to advancing health and well-being through innovative education, research, and community engagement. We prepare future public health leaders to address complex health challenges locally and globally.

Our Mission

To educate and train competent public health professionals equipped with knowledge, skills, and values to protect and promote the health of populations through evidence-based practices and community partnerships.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in public health education and research that contributes to healthier communities and sustainable development in Bangladesh and beyond.

Why Study Public Health at NSU?
  • Industry-experienced faculty with global recognition
  • Cutting-edge curriculum aligned with international standards
  • Research opportunities in emerging health challenges
  • Strong partnerships with local and international organizations
  • Career support and alumni network
